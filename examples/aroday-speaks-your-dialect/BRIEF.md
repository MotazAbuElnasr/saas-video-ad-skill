- **Angle (original):** Arabic speakers write the way they talk — «بكرة» in Cairo, «باچر» in the
  Gulf, «غدًا» in MSA. aro.day's quick-add understands all of them (src/lib/quick-parse-ar.ts).
- **Proof:** one real take, Arabic UI: «مكالمة العميل بكرة ٣ العصر لمدة ساعة» → a card set for
  tomorrow 15:00, one hour. A whole hour, not «نص ساعة» (user: "without halfs to make it simple").
