# aro.day — Speaks your dialect (v6, Egyptian Arabic, right to left)

Arabic look: the app in Arabic (RTL), catppuccin-latte, a kinetic hook whose faint words light up
as they are said (a highlighter drawn from the right, «غدًا»؟ landing with a shake), a zoom on the
input framed away from the app's untranslated strip, a plum payoff band that arrives with the card,
a dark lineup of five dialects' words for "tomorrow" (all parse in the app), a light end card.
Gemini Charon in Egyptian Arabic; the brand said in English ("arrow dot day", recorded as its own
segment at the start of line 2); captions right to left.

- `aroday-speaks-your-dialect.mp4` — the ad (720p preview; the master is 1080p, 8 Mbps, −14 LUFS).
- `ad.config.mjs`, `gen-frames.mjs` — RTL config: `look.dir`, `fonts.script`, `voice.lang`, `saidAs`.
- `BRIEF.md`, `STORYBOARD.md`, `SCRIPT.md` (with English glosses) — what was asked, planned, said.
- `capture/ad-arabic.scene.ts` — the video-demo scene (filmed from origin/main).

Lessons: 43–48 in `references/lessons.md`.
