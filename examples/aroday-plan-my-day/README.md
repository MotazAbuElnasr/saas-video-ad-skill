# aro.day — Day overview (v4)

Mocha look: the app's mocha theme at UI zoom 1.5 (a device setting, rendered sharp), a kinetic
hook — the headline waits in espresso outline (the thumbnail) and wipes in word by word as it is
said, "landed" hits with a shake, a highlighter swipes under "roadmap" — one eased push-in on the
strip on "one line", the real drag with a pop on the drop, an opaque espresso payoff card clear of
YouTube's Skip and ad-badge zones, a light end card composed from frame 0 that holds to the last
frame, cafe ambience. Gemini Charon, confident and friendly.

- `aroday-plan-my-day.mp4` — the ad (720p preview; the master is 1080p, 8 Mbps, −14 LUFS).
- `ad.config.mjs`, `gen-frames.mjs` — the project half (T2/T3: the cut at 02|03 skips the cursor's
  approach and the app's hover-card flash; the clash ring carries across it).
- `BRIEF.md`, `STORYBOARD.md`, `SCRIPT.md` — what was asked, planned and said.
- `capture/ad-plan.scene.ts` — the video-demo scene (a manual pointer drag with mirrored mouse
  events so the cursor follows the bar).
