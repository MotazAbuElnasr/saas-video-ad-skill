## Intent

Ad 5 — "Day overview, dragging" (user). In the app, "Day overview" is the board's today strip:
every scheduled task, meeting and due chip on one timeline, "drag a bar or ◆ to reschedule".
One continuous take of the real app: the client call (14:00–15:00) sits on the roadmap block
(14:00–15:30) → drag the roadmap bar to 15:00 → it fills the free 15:00–16:30 gap exactly
(90 minutes = its estimate) and the agenda cards below re-sort.

## Look — similar to the series, not the same (user)

- Footage in the app's own **mocha** theme (Cloud Dancer + Mocha Mousse, a tour theme), free
  tier, UI zoom 1.5 (the device setting) so the strip renders large and sharp.
- Wipe entrances; "highlighter" headline (espresso type, a mocha bar behind the key words) in
  the empty In Progress column; espresso cards; the first LIGHT end card of the series (the
  user rejected blue, not light) with a strip motif; a brook/stream bed.
- One eased push-in on the strip after the hook (nothing moves), held through the drag.

## Research applied

Hook ≤ 3s over the live app (the clash is the hook), brand said by ~3s, real UI, one message,
every claim visible (the clash, the 90-minute gap, the re-sorted agenda), captions on, CTA
said and shown, −14 LUFS.
