## Intent

Ad 4 — "Schedule for me" (user: "create one task then click on it schedule for me feature").
One continuous take of the real app: a full day (3 meetings, 5 tasks, 9:00–18:00) has exactly
one free hour left (17:00–18:00). Type "Prepare investor update 1h" → card menu → Schedule for
me (the menu already shows "Today 05:00 PM") → it lands in that hour on the day timeline.

## Look — similar to the series, not the same (user)

- Footage in the app's own **terminal** theme (dark, phosphor green) — one of the tour's
  curated themes; the other ads are pearl (light).
- Typewriter entrances; the empty In Progress column is the text zone (hook, subtitles,
  payoff card) — the app stays fully visible, no scrim; outline cards with a green glow.
- Setup → payoff in one spot: the hook rings the empty 17:00–18:00 slot; the payoff rings the
  task that lands in it.
- End card: near-black, typed tagline with a blinking block cursor, green CTA pill.
- Music: minimal ambient pulse, low (rule 16). Voice: Kokoro af_heart (free; user: free
  voices only, female is fine).

## Research applied (references/best-practices.md)

Hook ≤ 3s over live footage; brand said by ~3s and the app's logo on screen throughout; one
message; demo in a realistic case; every claim matches the screen ("one free hour" = the only
hour-long gap; "five p.m." = the slot it picks); captions on; CTA said and shown; −14 LUFS.
